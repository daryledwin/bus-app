Hi there! I’m Daryl, an SMU Software Engineering student. I started building MyBus SG over the summer as a way to explore app development and design. After using bus apps that often felt dated or cluttered, I wanted to create something more modern, calming, and commuter-friendly. Hopefully, this app makes everyday commutes feel just a little lighter.
